# Strength Lab: engine and transcript integration

This is an implementation map, not a claim that the whole archive is integrated.
Reproduce inventory with `node scripts/audit-curriculum.cjs` and operational routes
with `node scripts/audit-integration.cjs`. See TRAINING-RULES.md for current engine
contracts and research/*evidence.json for source facts versus app adaptations.

## What the counts mean

- 720 archived records are inventoried; 719 are usable under the supplied audit.
  Corrupt source #441 is excluded. Inventory does not mean programming integration.
- 54 movements have an operational engine route and dedicated instruction.
- 38 instruction outlines are reviewed within their stated scope. Sixteen retain

- Eight Book outlines remain separate and provisional. The unavailable master

  “all transcript knowledge integrated.”

## How knowledge becomes working training

1. **Instruction and provenance:** dedicated lessons describe the movement;
   evidence files distinguish transcript teaching, contextual examples and app rules.
2. **Admission:** explicit familiarity, recent qualifying supporting practice,
   owned equipment/pairs, station geometry, space and unresolved reviews are checked.
3. **Program coordination:** the chosen source block or rolling A/B/C/mixed blocks
   select eligible work under time, frequency, recovery and continuity constraints.
4. **Prescription:** a movement's own method supplies its dose. Different source
   methods keep independent comparison identities; the coordinator does not blend
   their numerical protocols into a fabricated universal system.
5. **Execution:** an immutable draft records the written target. Actual work is
   entered separately; elapsed timers never check sets or award training credit.
6. **Replay:** valid actual results determine progression, prerequisites and review
   eligibility. Reduced work has no ordinary advancement credit. Complex work does
   not credit its components. Future records do not supply current evidence.
7. **Continuity:** readiness, absence, incomplete returns, pain review, workload and
   missed work affect the next actual target. Corrections/undo replay the same rules.
8. **Persistence:** history, active drafts, backups, writer ownership, atomic saves
   and offline assets preserve the same model across reloads and corrections.

## Real prerequisite pathways, not a compulsory universal ladder

Examples below are actual practice edges in the registry. Every movement also
retains its own explicit skills/equipment gates; these are not promises of mastery.

| Supporting practice | Dependent work |
|---|---|
| Unloaded hinge | Reviewed deadlift practice |
| Two-hand swing | Hand-to-hand and introductory single-arm swing |
| Paired deadlift plus two-hand swing | Paired swing |
| Single press plus double clean | Double press |
| Single-rack squat plus double clean | Double squat |
| Two-hand hip drop | Single-hand hip drop |
| Bear crawl | Cat crawl |
| Double-club swing clean plus shield | Double-club pullover |
| Double-club swing clean plus pullover | Technical swipe |

A clean descendant may maintain previously earned prerequisite recency only along
registry-gated edges and within the existing evidence rules. It cannot invent
exposures or bypass a pending review. Familiarity and actual qualification remain
separate. Changing a method does not award work in another method.

## What recent reliability work accomplished

Settings and Train use shared saved controls and effective emphasis weights.
Weekly capacity also bounds forecasts. Missing load setup no longer consumes
real unfinished-work priority. Current methods handle reduced single-set doses,
full reduced-work delivery, unfinished returns and newly reviewed return work.
Changed-station light practice interrupts its comparison appropriately. Baseline
and configured-wave setting round trips no longer revive old comparison credit.

These fixes strengthen existing knowledge integration. They do not add reviewed
lessons or establish that every source program has been implemented.

## What still needs engineering and source review

1. **Sustainable curriculum composition:** oversized rotations can still produce
   repeated re-entry. Core work plus a manageable developing pathway needs further
   longitudinal evaluation at realistic time/frequency. Keep qualification intact.
2. **Fuller source-specific numerical systems:** additional double-club and
   bodyweight methods, broader paired-kettlebell programs and supported complexes
   need bounded source review plus complete execution/progression integration.
3. **Remaining movement branches:** outside/soft-style swing systems, broader
   paired/row/lunge paths, loaded stepping and additional get-up/complex branches
   are not implied by the current catalog's coverage.
4. **Instructional closure:** the sixteen partial lessons, visual technique checks,
   exact verified timestamps and physical Android/audio acceptance remain separate.
5. **Evidence accounting:** archive inventory and external completion labels cannot
   replace the missing source ledger or verified engine-level integration evidence.

Next work should increase supported programming depth and curriculum coherence,
while retaining the verified default behavior and user data. The coordinator now reserves opportunities for an explicitly selected core and
uses observed movement recurrence to account for deferred work. Protected-core
plans obey the selected total duration. This remains an app capacity policy;
the diagnostic histories do not certify sustainable mixed curriculum coverage.
It also reports program-day recurrence under a simple ready-program
rotation, effective weekly capacity and observed pace. This can expose an A/B/C
day returning every 21 days despite weekly block visits. It is early planning
feedback, not automatic curriculum curation or an exercise-level feasibility
guarantee; internal pattern rotation and future eligibility still need work.
Automation uses
synthetic histories and browser emulation; it does not certify physical technique.
