## Audit questions before declaring research fidelity

1. Is each referenced source among the fully reviewed 504, partially reviewed, or still unreviewed? Cross-check against durable research ledger, not source numbering.
2. Does each exact-parameter source actually specify the quoted load, sets, reps, rest, cadence, and context? Record direct evidence anchors.
3. Are prerequisites and movement pathways grounded in technical instruction, or are they conservative app assumptions? Label each accordingly.
4. Verify the 7 partial/pending instruction entries against source material and add complete setup, movement, errors, and safety limits where supported.
5. Audit app-defined numerical defaults: 2 qualifying exposures, 14-day reentry, fractions for reduced readiness and reentry, stall logic, deload intervals, training-day heuristics, and time-budget scaling.
6. Validate every pathway against actual equipment availability and regression tests. Do not automatically certify technique based on completion logs.
7. Compare against remaining 216 transcripts once reviewed; log material contradictions and omissions before changing engine policy.
