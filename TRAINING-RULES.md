# Training-rule implementation map

The authoritative prescriptions are `engine.js` and each movement's
`policyFor` provenance. Source facts, contextual examples and app decisions are
separate in `research/*evidence.json`. This document does not establish new source
support or certify technique.

## Evidence and advancement

- Actual results remain separate from the written prescription.
- Missing, skipped, unequal-sided, painful or technically failed work cannot earn
  advancement. Reduced return/recovery/time-fit work has no full-dose credit.
- Different movements and implements have independent cycles. Complex records
  do not award component progression. Explicit skill confirmation is separate
  from repetitions.
- Loading uses configured owned equipment; paired movements require matched
  inventory and record load per bell/club. Starting loads and later increases
  require explicit review. Source percentage examples are not universal conversions.
- Historical corrections replay progression and eligibility; old prescriptions
  and correction/undo history are retained. Timers never prove physical completion.

## Program composition: app decisions

Coordinated blocks retain their own source methods. Selection, spacing, workload
caps, recency windows and follow-up priority are configurable Strength Lab rules,
not a complete implementation of #548's full system. No endurance activity is
converted into fictional strength volume.

Zero-load starting-weight placeholders are setup needs, not executable work or
unfinished training debt. Coordinated plans omit them while retaining setup
reasons. Genuine partial/skipped prescribed work still carries forward. Existing
single-block setup behavior and historical records remain compatible.

Recent qualified descendant practice can maintain prerequisite recency only along
actual registry prerequisite edges. Every prerequisite must already have its own
clean evidence at the current policy/load, required skills and no unresolved pain
or newer failed work. The recent descendant also needs actual qualifying practice
and explicit required skills. Multi-level maintenance does not add foundation
repetitions, progression, load, mastery or future workout completion. Unrelated
movements and educational-only links cannot maintain eligibility. Corrections,
load/policy changes, failure and absence can require requalification.

Oversized rotations may still exceed a sustainable cadence. Warnings and bounded
follow-ups do not manufacture eligibility or add catch-up work.

## Persistence and delivery

Keep V33 storage keys, writer ownership, atomic state/draft transactions, backup
validation and correction replay intact. Eight Books stay separate from the
Movement Library. The preview is distinct from production; passing automation
is not physical Android or human technique verification.

## Hand-change practice

Source #185 supplies alternating hand-change instruction and two-hand swing
review, but no numerical program or compulsory breath cadence. The swingSkill
profile uses app-selected 3–5 reps per driving hand, 2–4 default sets, 90-second
rest, owned single-bell load review and explicit skill/recent-practice gates.
Five per hand means ten alternating swings total. Unequal/missing side counts
never earn completion credit. This cycle does not advance two-hand swings or
create automatic eligibility for paired kettlebell movements.


## Introductory single-arm swing

Source #67 teaches thumb-down/back, small swings, low introductory arm path,
inhale down/exhale up and controlled parking. #446 distinguishes hard-style
and soft-style endurance as separate skills. This new opt-in path covers the
introduction only. Confirm familiarity explicitly and retain recent qualified
two-hand practice. Complete one side, park, then repeat on the other; actual
bilateral counts are required. Sets, 3–5 reps per side, rest, planning pace,
evidence gates and load limits are Strength Lab app defaults. Existing
hand-change/paired gates and histories do not acquire a new prerequisite or
component progression. No source numerical cycle or technique mastery is claimed.


## Dated physical evidence

Prescriptions and prerequisite checks use records through the requested training
date (today when omitted). Later-dated imports/corrections remain in Training Log
but do not add current credit, failure counts, wave turns or supporting practice,
or erase an existing absence. Known unresolved pain is still held for review.
Date correction/undo replays the same original prescriptions and actuals.

Dated load review cannot qualify from later work. A valid later load-reset anchor
is retained and does not apply its pending increase before the anchor date;
a genuinely missing anchor still blocks replay. Explicit current settings and
unanchored starting loads stay current—no historical settings are invented.
Unscoped engine record/state inspection still exposes the full retained archive;
the current interface and dated actions request today's evidence explicitly.

Future unfinished work cannot replace real earlier debt. Existing explicit/legacy
pending work is preserved unless it is introduced solely by future unfinished
records; earlier physical debt is reconstructed from earlier written plans and
actuals. Forecasts freeze evidence at their starting date, even when displaying
later opportunities. Empty internal ordering placeholders can turn a provisional
slot but cannot earn progression, recency, mastery or load advancement. These
are application data/selection rules, not new transcript prescriptions.
